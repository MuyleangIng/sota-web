# Sota សោតៈ · Khmer speech recognition

Website: **https://muyleanging.github.io/sota-web/**

Sota ("ear") is a small Khmer + English speech recognition model in development by Ing Muyleang: it will turn
voice notes, meetings, lectures and phone calls into text, offline on your phone. The site shows the planned
architecture (three figures), the benchmark plan, the training data and the roadmap.

From the maker of [Akkhara](https://muyleanging.github.io/akkhara-web/) and [Chakkhu](https://muyleanging.github.io/chakkhu-web/):
Akkhara types, Chakkhu reads, Sota listens.
